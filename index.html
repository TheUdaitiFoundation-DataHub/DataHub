Highway access

The clusters' 5 km areas are crossed by 13 highway and expressway routes. Five pass through the area around Khopoli–Khalapur, including the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.

Proximity to Skilling Opportunities

Only 1 of 18 mapped ITIs lies within a cluster buffer — ITI Khalapur. Most training capacity sits outside the 5 km catchments.

Village catchments

243 villages touch a buffer and 113 lie fully inside one. Dolvi–Pen reaches the most villages (59) with just three mapped units.
